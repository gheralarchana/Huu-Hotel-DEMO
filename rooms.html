Important Information

Stay & Booking Policies

schedule

Check-in & Check-out Times

Check-in begins at 14:00 WITA and check-out is until 12:00 WITA. Complimentary secure luggage storage is available should you arrive early via fast boat or wish to savor the beach after checking out.

event_busy

Flexible 7-Day Free Cancellation

We understand tropical travel plans may evolve. Enjoy full penalty-free cancellation up to 7 calendar days prior to your arrival date.

smoke_free

Smoke-Free Indoor Policy & Island Quiet Hours

All interior room spaces are strictly non-smoking to preserve pure island air. Designated smoking is permitted on outdoor balconies and designated open beach areas.

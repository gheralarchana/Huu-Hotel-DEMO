Getting Here

Journey to HU'U Sanctuary, Gili Trawangan

Gili Trawangan is an eco-conscious island free from engine noise and exhaust. Your island journey begins with soft sea breezes and traditional island transport: vintage push bikes or horse-drawn Cidomo carriages.

1

Speedboat from Lombok / Bali

A swift 15-minute speed boat crossing from Teluk Nara or Bangsal Harbour in Lombok, or 2 hours from Padang Bai, Bali.

2

Scenic Ride to Resort by Cidomo carriage or bicycle

From the main harbour, take a charming 10-minute Cidomo ride or leisurely cycle along the picturesque western coastal trail.

3

Concierge Luggage Pick-up

HU'U's harbor concierge team warmly greets you upon arrival at Gili port to handle your luggage with effortless care.
